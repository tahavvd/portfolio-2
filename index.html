Inventory Management System

A clean, role-based inventory management system for small businesses. Stock, orders, suppliers, and warehouses — all in one place.

Problem

Manual inventory tracking made stock levels hard to trust and errors easy to miss.

Solution

A single operations system with roles, stock movement, order history, and warehouse visibility.

Outcome

Fewer stock mistakes, a clear audit trail, and more control over daily operations.

  • Real-time stock tracking per product and warehouse
  • Automatic stock deduction and restoration on cancellation
  • Full stock transaction audit trail with filters
  • Order lifecycle and history management
  • Supplier-linked stock intake per warehouse
  • Admin and employee access levels

Salon Appointment Booking System

A real-time booking system built to replace a paper notebook that was causing double-bookings and scheduling chaos.

Problem

The salon owner relied on paper and often booked two clients into the same slot.

Solution

A phone-friendly booking flow with independent stylist schedules and live availability.

In progress

The client-facing flow is complete; the owner and staff admin dashboard is still being built.

  • Clients book by name and phone, with no account required
  • Services show photos, descriptions, price, and duration
  • Availability accounts for bookings and full service duration
  • Server checks prevent two clients taking one slot
  • Clients can view or cancel upcoming appointments
  • Built around a high-end salon experience

The system will not let two clients book the same slot, even when they choose it at the same time.
